Lawful rules

Independent project by Ajlal Khan. Not Qiwa, Absher, MHRSD, Taqat or Jadarat. Information here is not legal advice.

What we are

A matching board. Job seekers browse and apply free. Employers request a listing. A vacancy is not public until a human reviews the company. Official employment contracts must be completed on Qiwa and other government systems. We cannot issue visas, Iqama, or work permits.

What we are not

We are not a government portal. We do not replace licensed recruitment offices where the law requires a licence. Launch phase: SAMPLE cards are fake vacancies for testing the search. They are labelled SAMPLE.

Money

Seekers: 0 SAR to us. Employers: launch price 199 SAR per reviewed listing. We do not take a percentage of a worker’s salary. Anyone asking a seeker to pay “to get the job” is breaking our rules — report them.

Privacy

Applications store name, WhatsApp, city and note. Employer forms store company, CR, email and job text. Do not upload passport or Iqama photos on this website. Do not send bank passwords.

AI Care

Ajlal AI Care gives general orientation (scam signs, CV tips, public Qiwa/Iqama basics). It is not a lawyer, not HRSD, and can be wrong. Trust qiwa.sa and hrsd.gov.sa.
